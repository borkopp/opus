## Design Context

### Users
Professional business owners and managers in the service industry (salons, barbershops, fitness) who need a central "Operating System" to manage their entire business, from bookings to payments.

### Brand Personality
**Premium, Precise, Empathetic**. The interface should evoke a sense of professional control while remaining approachable and modern.

### Aesthetic Direction
High-end modern SaaS dashboard. Uses a refined typography stack (Outfit/Display fonts), a clean color palette with high-contrast primary accents, and subtle glassmorphic elements (translucency, border-play). The layout is organized into purposeful "widgets" with consistent 24px/16px radii.

### Design Principles
1. **Subtle Sophistication**: Use purposeful micro-interactions and animations to guide the eye without overwhelming the user.
2. **Insight Over Information**: Prioritize clarity of data; numbers should feel significant and easy to parse at a glance.
3. **Tactile Digitalism**: Elements should feel interactive through subtle depth, scale changes, and physics-based transitions.
4. **Consistent Elegance**: Maintain strict adherence to defined spacing scales and typography to ensure a premium feel across all modules.
