### Shared art direction

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a portrait photograph for a premium beauty-studio software website's 'Who it is for' gallery, part of a coordinated six-photo editorial series. Create one photograph only, portrait 4:5 composition, no collage. Art direction: an intimate, beautifully kept small European neighborhood studio, quiet understated luxury rather than a giant luxury resort. Warm late-afternoon natural window light, gentle directional shadows, rich but softly lifted shadows, honest skin and material texture, subtle fine film grain. Palette: warm cream, oatmeal linen, walnut wood, muted cognac, brushed antique brass, a small terracotta accent. Modern editorial lifestyle photography on a 50mm lens, shallow but not extreme depth of field, relaxed candid moment, very natural proportions. Keep the important service and people in the central 70% so a slightly tighter portrait crop works. Show enough of the setting to instantly identify this type of business. No typography, no legible text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI, no frames, no stock-photo posing, no exaggerated orange filter, no plastic skin, no distorted fingers or tools.
