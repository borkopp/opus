## Design Context

### Users
End customers in Macedonia (primarily Skopje) searching for and booking local service businesses — barbers, salons, spas, consultants, trainers. Mobile-first; most will arrive on a phone. They want frictionless discovery and quick booking, not a feature-heavy experience.

### Brand Personality
Easy-to-use · Minimalistic · Modern

Tone: confident and refined without being cold. Warm, approachable, fast. Airbnb/Uber as reference points — functional elegance, no decorative excess.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Light mode primary** with full dark mode adaptation
- Warm earthy palette: clay CTA (`--cta` oklch 47% 0.20 30), warm amber ratings, earthy green success states
- Typography: Syne (display/headings) + DM Sans (body) — already established in the system
- Generous white space on mobile; no card nesting; flat information hierarchy
- Anti-references: glossy fintech apps, dark-glass dashboards, neon/glow accents, purple gradients

### Design Principles
1. **Mobile first, always** — max-w-3xl, thumb-zone CTAs, sticky booking button
2. **Warm minimalism** — the palette is earthy and restrained; avoid cold grays and pure blacks
3. **Friction-zero** — every interaction should feel instant; show data, hide mechanics
4. **Typography does the lifting** — Syne for character, DM Sans for clarity; no icon+heading combos
5. **No card soup** — sections are separated by thin Separators and space, not nested cards
